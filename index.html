Guitar, bass & ukulele lessons from someone who genuinely loves this

Hi, I'm Richard. I've taught in schools, coached musicians who now tour internationally, and helped plenty of kids land their first proper chord. These days I teach guitar, bass and ukulele online from my own studio — every age, every level, and none of that school-style rigidity.

Expect the odd Star Wars quote and Garfield joke between scales. And if I ever pause for a biscuit mid-lesson, that's just the type 1 diabetes — I'll always tell you if I need a minute.

Why learn with me?

I'll be straight with you: learning an instrument — and the music theory that goes with it — is difficult. I'm not going to dress that up. But a good teacher helps you find the shortcuts, and that's exactly what I do. I don't do prescriptive, school-style lessons; I'm personable, I listen, and I show you the quickest route to playing the songs you actually want to play.

🎼

Your songs, tabbed out properly

Got a tune you're obsessed with? I'll transcribe it for you — accurate tab, in a key and arrangement that fits your hands. I've tabbed out some properly obscure requests over the years, and I love it.

📝

Grade exams, minus the fear

I've sat on the other side of the exam room, so I know exactly what ABRSM, Rockschool and Trinity examiners are listening for. I'll get you genuinely ready — confident, not crammed.

🎙️

You'll hear every note properly

Good mic, good interface, clean picture — I built my setup so online lessons actually sound like we're in the same room. Hard to fix your picking if you can't hear it clearly.
