About Richard

About Richard

An experienced guitarist and former school teacher, Richard makes online lessons feel personal, relaxed and genuinely enjoyable — for every age and every level.

Teaching philosophy

Lessons should be the highlight of your week. Richard believes the best progress happens when you're playing music you love, at a pace that feels right, with a teacher who listens.

Every student gets a personal plan — whether that's nailing a grade exam, mastering a favourite song, or simply building confidence on the instrument.

  • 🤝Patient & encouraging — no judgement, ever.
  • 🎯Goal-driven — clear direction in every lesson.
  • 📈Progress you can feel — from the very first lesson.

A studio-quality online setup

Great lessons need great sound. Richard teaches from a dedicated studio setup so you hear every note with absolute clarity — just as if he were in the room.
