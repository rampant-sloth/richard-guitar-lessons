About Richard

About Richard

Guitarist, former school teacher, and the bloke on the other end of the Zoom call. Here's a bit about me and how I teach.

How I teach

I think lessons should be the best part of your week — not a chore you dread. You learn fastest when you're playing music you love, at a pace that feels right, with a teacher who actually listens. So that's what I do.

Learning an instrument and music theory is difficult — I'll never pretend otherwise. But a good teacher helps you find the shortcuts, and that's my whole job. Not prescriptive, not school-style; just personable, honest and focused on getting you playing.

When I'm not teaching, I'm probably watching Star Wars, re-reading a Garfield comic, or keeping an eye on my blood sugar — I'm type 1 diabetic, so if I ever need a minute mid-lesson, I'll just say.

  • 🤝Patient & encouraging — no judgement, ever.
  • 🎯Goal-driven — clear direction in every lesson.
  • 📈Progress you can feel — from the very first lesson.
