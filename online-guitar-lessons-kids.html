Structured learning that builds real progress

Children learn best with a clear, consistent structure. Each lesson follows a steady, age-appropriate path — from first chords and reading tab, through to scales, technique and confident performance.

Short, focused tasks keep younger learners engaged, with simple practice targets set every week so progress stays visible and rewarding.

  • 🎯Clear weekly goals — every lesson has a simple, achievable target.
  • 📚Age-appropriate resources — fun songs and exercises matched to each child.
  • 🏆Recognised grade paths — ABRSM, Rockschool and Trinity exam support.

Safeguarding you can trust

As a former school teacher, Richard is fully committed to child safety. Lessons are delivered with clear online-safety practices, and parents or guardians are always welcome to be present.
